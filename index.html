Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our services and practice.

I specialize in several key areas of mental health counseling:

  • Trauma Counseling: Working with individuals who have experienced traumatic events, using evidence-based approaches to process difficult memories and develop healthy coping strategies.
  • Grief Counseling: Supporting clients through the loss of loved ones, helping them navigate the complex emotions of grief and find meaningful ways to honor their loss while moving forward.
  • Personality Disorder Counseling: Providing compassionate care for individuals dealing with personality disorders, focusing on building self-awareness, improving relationships, and developing emotional regulation skills.
  • EMDR Therapy: Utilizing Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing, a specialized technique proven effective for treating trauma, PTSD, anxiety, and other distressing life experiences.

The standard fee for individual therapy sessions is $185 per session. Each session is typically 50-60 minutes in length.

I understand that financial circumstances vary, and I believe everyone deserves access to quality mental health care. In cases of financial need, I offer some flexibility with my fees. Please don't hesitate to discuss your situation with me during our initial consultation.

I do not accept insurance directly or bill insurance companies on your behalf. However, many clients are able to receive reimbursement for out-of-network mental health services through their insurance plans.

I am happy to provide you with a detailed receipt (superbill) that includes all the necessary information for you to submit to your insurance company for potential reimbursement. I recommend contacting your insurance provider to verify your out-of-network mental health benefits before our first session.
