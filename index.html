Meet Twyla Axelson

I am a Licensed Professional Counselor serving North Denton County and the Lake Lewisville area. I specialize in trauma, anxiety, relationship struggles, and the lasting impact of family-of-origin experiences.

I work with individuals, couples, and families who feel stuck in patterns they can’t seem to break — overthinking, shutting down in conflict, avoiding difficult conversations, or carrying stress that never really turns off. Patterns that once made sense may now be creating distance, tension, or exhaustion.

If something keeps happening in your life or relationships, it’s not random. Patterns are learned over time. When we understand how one was built, we can adjust it — instead of staying stuck in it — and build something better.

My style is warm, steady, and direct. In our work together, we slow things down and make sense of what’s happening beneath the surface. Clients often tell me they feel comfortable being honest here — even about the messy or hard-to-say things. My goal is for you to feel truly known, not just treated — because that’s where meaningful change begins.

Services

Specialized care for your mental health journey.

Individual Therapy

Support for adults navigating anxiety, attachment wounds, trauma, and life transitions. Together, we examine how past experiences are shaping current patterns and build practical tools to feel more secure, grounded, and confident in your life and relationships.

Couples Therapy

For couples experiencing conflict, disconnection, or simply wanting to strengthen their relationship. We identify the patterns driving tension, understand how each partner’s history influences the dynamic, and build healthier ways of communicating, repairing, and reconnecting.

Group Therapy

Group therapy offers a steady, supportive space to connect with others who are doing similar work. You’ll gain insight into relational patterns, practice new ways of engaging, and experience growth in real time — not just through insight, but through interaction.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about the practice and its services.

I specialize in several key areas of mental health counseling:

  • Trauma Recovery: Helping you unpack and process past experiences so you can feel more grounded, secure, and confident in your daily life.
  • Navigating Grief: Providing a supportive space to work through the weight of loss and find a sustainable and authentic way to move forward.
  • Family Systems: Working with multiple members of a family system or couple to explore patterns and help break old cycles to build healthier, more meaningful relationships.
  • EMDR Therapy: A specialized, brain-based approach designed to help you "unstick" painful memories and find lasting relief from distress.
  • Eating Disorder Therapy: Supportive step down from higher levels of care (IOP/PHP) to deepen your recovery, moving beyond stabilization to explore the root issues, reclaim your identity, and foster a peaceful relationship with your body and food.

I primarily work with adults and couples. I am also trained in working with children and teens and provide thoughtful and developmentally appropriate therapy for ages 4 and up when it makes clinical sense — particularly as part of a larger family system approach.

The standard fee for individual and couples therapy sessions is $185 per session. Each session is typically 50-60 minutes in length.

Costs for group therapy sessions are generally $75 per 90 minute session.

I understand that financial circumstances vary, and I believe everyone deserves access to quality mental health care. In cases of financial need, I offer some flexibility with my fees. Please don't hesitate to discuss your situation with me during our initial consultation.

I do not accept insurance directly or bill insurance companies on your behalf. However, many clients are able to receive reimbursement for out-of-network mental health services through their insurance plans.

I am happy to provide you with a detailed receipt (superbill) that includes all the necessary information for you to submit to your insurance company for potential reimbursement. I recommend contacting your insurance provider to verify your out-of-network mental health benefits before our first session.

Alternatively, I partner with Mentaya, a third-party organization which allows me to check your out-of-network benefits. Many clients have achievable out-of-network deductibles and are able to be reimbursed for a percentage of their session fee once those are met. If you are interested in learning more about this, let me know during our free consultation.
